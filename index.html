Charleston Polo Club

“Let others play at other things The King of Games is still the Game of Kings”
Thomas Osbert Mordaunt

A new chapter in the sporting life of the Holy City, welcoming riders, patrons, horsemen, and guests into the enduring fellowship of polo.

  1. I

    Beginner Instruction

    Embark upon your polo journey through carefully guided schooling sessions designed to establish sound horsemanship, a secure seat, and the foundations of the game.

  2. II

    Practice Chukkers

    Hire one of our experienced equine companions for structured practice chukkers, instructional play, and developing games.

  3. III

    CPC Coached League

    Claim your number in our coached league and progress from schooling sessions into organized play within a supportive club structure.

Open to New Players

No previous experience is required. Necessary equipment, playing gear, and a suitable experienced horse are provided for the beginning of your polo journey.
