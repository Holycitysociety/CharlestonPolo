Cowboy Polo Circuit

What was born in the South as Palmetto Polo became the Cowboy Polo Circuit, Charleston Polo’s USPPA development pathway for new riders, horses, and local competition. As Cowboy Polo approaches its 75th anniversary in 2027, Charleston Polo is helping preserve this distinctly American lineage of the sport— connecting its history to a practical new route into the King of Games.
